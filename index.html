Privacy Policy for Pro Shorts

Last updated: 25 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Pro Shorts (the "Game"), developed by Tarkwn ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it on Android. By installing and playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do and do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact details. Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to us in a form that identifies you.

To understand and improve gameplay, the Game does collect anonymous, aggregated analytics about how levels are played (described in Section 2 below). The Game also uses third-party services for analytics, advertising, and in-app purchases, which may automatically collect certain information as described in this policy.

2. Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players experience the Game so we can balance difficulty and fix problems. This analytics data is aggregated and not used to identify you personally. It may include:

  • Gameplay events such as starting, completing, failing, or quitting a level, and for each level the number of moves and the time spent;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • Approximate location (country) derived from IP address;
  • A pseudonymous app-instance identifier and, on Android, device identifiers such as the Advertising ID, used by the analytics service to count unique installs and sessions.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf. We do not collect your name, email, or precise location through analytics. For more information see Firebase Privacy & Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation (which includes Unity Ads / ironSource and may mediate other ad networks). To deliver and measure ads, these providers may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / AD_ID);
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, network type);
  • Approximate location derived from IP address;
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions).

This data is used to show ads, limit ad frequency, measure performance, and (where permitted) personalize ads. We do not control how these third parties use the data; please review their policies:

  • Unity / Unity LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google (Advertising ID & Play services): policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details. Google processes the transaction and shares with us only confirmation of the purchase so we can grant the purchased item. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

Pro Shorts is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under the age defined by applicable law (e.g., 13 under COPPA, 16 under GDPR). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the Game, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it also limits the identifiers available to analytics and advertising partners.
  • Analytics: The analytics we collect is anonymous and aggregated. You can further limit Google's processing through your device and Google account privacy settings.
  • Consent (EEA/UK): Where required, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time within the Game.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK): You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing.
  • CCPA (California): You have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information.
  • To clear locally stored progress, you can uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.

7. Data retention & security

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete it. Analytics, advertising, and payment data handled by our partners is retained according to their respective policies. We rely on these established providers' security practices to protect data processed through their services.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
